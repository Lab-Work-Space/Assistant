## 1. Defect
**DEFECT-001 Medium, owner backend.** `core/mcp/ext.py:44-47`: the wrapped `recover()` calls `gw.recover()` on every call, while `approvals.recover` is once-per-process (`core/approvals/service.py:377`, `_recovered`). Discord `on_ready` re-fires on reconnect, so an irreversible replay that is in flight (`state='claimed'`) is flipped to `unknown` (`core/mcp/replay.py:109`) and logged ERROR `mcp.replay_unknown`; the human sees a wrong state, and `stale`/replay sweeps re-run mid-flight. Atomic claim still prevents a double call.
Repro: `tests/acceptance_mcp/test_mcp_e2e.py::test_reconnect_recover_does_not_flip_inflight_claim` (xfail strict; `--runxfail` gives `assert 'unknown' == 'claimed'`). Fix: run `gw.recover()` once, inside the `started` guard.
