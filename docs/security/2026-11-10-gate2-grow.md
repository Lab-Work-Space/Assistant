## Rulings on deviations

All are **accepted**:
- **BE1.**
  - Dropping `outcome IS NULL` is what GW-C6 asked for.
  - The F2 origin filter is GW-C5.
  - Closing `awaiting_approval` is stricter.
  - The single `Promise.all` is awaited before the reset (fail mode: G2-GW-07).
  - Non-DST day arithmetic in `retroSlot` is safe for Asia/Bangkok and for hour ≥ 1. `from` is display only.
- **BE2.**
  - The offer marker narrows F-6 (residual G2-GW-05).
  - The GW-C10 split is right: a create has no active version.
  - AI `gone` with no audit row matches "writes nothing".
  - Notice only while open: a card or decision already reached a person.
  - `skill.ai.apply` for ok and refused rows cannot inflate the cap: the count filters "`outcome LIKE 'claim %'`" and dept via `tool = $2`, and those rows carry `tool = proposal:N`.
  - Template names in the URL check are stricter.
  - The `ai_review` line on the `/skill review` card is labelled "sanitised, not authoritative" and is allowed by C13.
- **BE3.**
  - The in-tx GW-C21 check is stricter.
  - `setIf` invalid → void writes nothing.
  - The narrowed `PlanDeps` is type-only.
  - The SAFE_TOOL_NAME skip and the service closing card and notice are both fine.
  - Screening the idea name is stricter.
  - Refusing a masked problem under 10 characters keeps the DB CHECK from throwing.
  - Boot reconcile from `applying` is GW-C20 (race: G2-GW-06).
